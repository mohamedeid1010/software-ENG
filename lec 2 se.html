Sheet 2 · Q1

Characteristics of Good Software Slides 7–9

A software product is judged by what it offers and how well it's used. It's measured on three grounds: Operational, Transitional, Maintenance.

① Operational — how well it works while running

BudgetUsabilityEfficiencyCorrectness FunctionalityDependabilitySecuritySafety

② Transitional — when moved from one platform to another

PortabilityInteroperabilityReusabilityAdaptability

③ Maintenance — how well it maintains itself in a changing world

ModularityMaintainabilityFlexibilityScalability
Think of a good backpack: Operational = it carries your books well right now. Transitional = it still works if you move to a new school / new country. Maintenance = you can fix a zipper or add pockets easily over time. Good software needs all three.
سؤال شبه مؤكد: اشرح خصائص السوفتوير الجيد من حيث Operational / Transitional / Maintenance. احفظ الكلمات اللي تحت كل واحدة (مش لازم كلها، بس عارف الفكرة):
• Operational = شغّال دلوقتي كويس (usability, correctness, security, safety…).
• Transitional = ينتقل بين المنصّات (portability, reusability…).
• Maintenance = يتصان ويتطوّر بسهولة (modularity, scalability…).

In short: Software engineering is a branch of computer science using well-defined engineering concepts to produce efficient, durable, scalable, in-budget, on-time software products.

Software Engineering Ethics Slides 14–17

SE involves wider responsibilities than just technical skill. Engineers must behave honestly & ethically. Ethical behaviour is more than just obeying the law — it means following morally correct principles.

The ACM/IEEE Code of Ethics — 8 Principles

US professional societies (ACM + IEEE) created this code. Members sign it. It has 8 principles. Mnemonic: P-C-P-J-M-P-C-S.

1️⃣
PUBLIC

Act consistently with the public interest.

2️⃣
CLIENT & EMPLOYER

Act in their best interest — consistent with the public interest.

3️⃣
PRODUCT

Products & modifications meet the highest professional standards.

4️⃣
JUDGMENT

Maintain integrity & independence in professional judgment.

5️⃣
MANAGEMENT

Managers & leaders promote an ethical approach to management.

6️⃣
PROFESSION

Advance the integrity & reputation of the profession.

7️⃣
COLLEAGUES

Be fair to and supportive of colleagues.

8️⃣
SELF

Commit to lifelong learning & promote an ethical approach.

🧠 8 principles order → "Please Call Patrick, Just Make Professional Colleagues Smile"

Issues of Professional Responsibility

Confidentiality

Respect employer/client confidentiality — even without a signed agreement.

Competence

Don't misrepresent your skill; don't accept work beyond your competence.

Intellectual Property

Know local laws (patents, copyright); protect employers'/clients' IP.

Computer Misuse

Don't misuse others' computers — from trivial (gaming at work) to serious (spreading viruses).

Ethical Dilemmas (slide 17)

⚖️
Disagreement

Disagreeing in principle with senior management's policies.

🧪
Untested release

Employer acts unethically — releases a safety-critical system without finishing testing.

💣
Weapons

Being asked to participate in military weapons or nuclear systems.

أهم نقطة للأخلاقيات: الكود بيتبع ACM/IEEE وفيه 8 مبادئ — وأهم مبدأ لو اتسألت عن «الأولوية وقت تعارض المصالح» = مصلحة العامة (public health, safety, welfare) ومصلحة العميل/المستخدم. وممنوع تحطّ مصلحتك الشخصية فوق الكل.

The 4 Case Studies Slides 18–41

The book uses these example systems. For each: know its meaning, its block diagram, and the key arguments/concerns.

① Insulin Pump Control System 💉

Meaning: an embedded, safety-critical system inside an insulin pump for diabetics. It reads blood sugar and injects the right insulin dose automatically.

  • Collects data from a blood sugar sensor, calculates insulin needed (based on the rate of change of sugar).
  • Sends signals to a micro-pump to deliver the correct dose.
  • Safety-critical: low sugar → coma/death; high sugar → long-term eye & kidney damage.
Slide 20 — Hardware architecture (Textbook Version)
Slide 21 — Activity model (Textbook Version)
It's like a smart thermostat for your blood: it reads the level, decides how much "fix" to inject, and acts — but because a mistake can kill, it must be extremely reliable.

② MHC-PMS — Mental Health Care Patient Management System 🏥

Meaning: a medical information system that stores info about mental-health patients and their treatments. Used in clinics, hospitals, and community centres.

  • Uses a centralized patient database, but also runs on a PC so it works at sites without secure network (download local copies when disconnected).
  • Goals: generate management info for health-service targets + give medical staff timely info to support treatment.
  • Key features: Individual care management · Patient monitoring (warnings) · Administrative reporting.
Slide 26 — Organization of the MHC-PMS (Textbook Version)

Concerns (slide 28)

🔒
Privacy

Patient info must be confidential — never disclosed to anyone except authorised staff & the patient.

🚨
Safety

Some illnesses make patients suicidal/dangerous → system should warn staff. Must be available or care is compromised.

③ Wilderness Weather Station 🌦️

Meaning: a data-collection system. A government deploys hundreds of stations in remote areas to measure temperature, pressure, sunshine, rainfall, wind speed & direction over 24-hour periods.

Weather station system

Collects weather data, does initial processing, transmits it to the management system.

Data management & archiving

Collects data from all stations, processes, analyses & archives it.

Station maintenance

Communicates by satellite to monitor station health & report problems.

Additional functionality: monitor instruments & report faults · manage power (charge batteries, shut generators in high wind) · support dynamic reconfiguration (swap in backup instruments / new software versions).

④ iLearn — Digital Learning Environment 📚

Meaning: a service-oriented educational framework. Teachers & learners pick the tools they need (spreadsheets, VLE for homework, games, simulations). Every component is a replaceable service, so it updates incrementally and can be configured for different groups (young kids, senior students).

Service integration: Integrated services offer an API (direct service-to-service talk). Independent services are accessed only through the browser; sharing needs explicit user actions like copy/paste, and may require re-authentication.

⚡ Last-Minute Cram Sheet

Read this in the 30 minutes before the exam.

The 12 facts you must not lose marks on
  • Software = programs + data + documentation (system & user).
  • SE = engineering discipline → all aspects of software production.
  • 4 SE activities = Specification · Development · Validation · Evolution.
  • 4 good-software attributes = Maintainability · Dependability & Security · Efficiency · Acceptability.
  • 3 design concerns = Time · Cost · Quality (the triangle).
  • Costs ≈ 60% development, 40% testing; evolution often > development.
  • 3 crises = Scotland flights 2009 · Ariane 5 (overflow, 36.7s) · London Ambulance 1992 (46 deaths).
  • 8 app types incl. stand-alone, embedded (most common), systems of systems.
  • Good software grounds = Operational · Transitional · Maintenance.
  • Ethics = ACM/IEEE, 8 principles; priority = public health, safety & welfare.
  • Cases = Insulin pump (embedded/medical, safety-critical) · MHC-PMS (info, privacy+safety) · Weather station (data collection) · iLearn (educational, service-oriented).
  • SE diversity = no universal set of techniques for all software.
